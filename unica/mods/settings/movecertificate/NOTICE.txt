Move Certificate by ys1231 — UN1CA ROM adaptation
Upstream: https://github.com/ys1231/MoveCertificate
Upstream license: Apache License 2.0

The original module's temporary certificate-store mount approach and AdGuard
intermediate exclusion informed this independently implemented ROM port.
The ROM port replaces Magisk/KernelSU/APatch scripts and WebUI with a native
init worker and a Settings activity. Only explicitly imported certificates
are promoted; Android user certificates are never moved or deleted.
