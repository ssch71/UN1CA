  native-depth-probe uses the stock public InitEx(1,4), SetCaliData,
  SetCameraImageInfo, CalcDisparityData and GetDisparityData APIs. Exact-engine
  Offscreen plane offsets16/pitch48 and public param orientation/face fields
  were traced in REA. Format0x802 is the semi-planar VU input branch; both Y and
  UV pointers reference actual complete NV21 captures. No fabricated face list.
  Isolated process has a60-second alarm/core dumps off. ABI errors may crash or
  fail that helper; they cannot become a successful camera/depth verdict.
  Native return codes/stdout/stderr are retained. Only successful0x701 output

  Codes are8-bit proprietary disparity, not calibrated physical distance.
